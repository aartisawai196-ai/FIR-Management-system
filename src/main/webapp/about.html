About FIR Management System

The FIR Management System is designed to streamline the process of filing, tracking, and generating reports for First Information Reports (FIRs).

This system provides users with the ability to search FIRs using Aadhaar or FIR number, track the status of their complaints, and generate official reports quickly.

Our goal is to make the FIR process transparent, efficient, and user-friendly for citizens and law enforcement agencies alike.
